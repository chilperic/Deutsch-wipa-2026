# Comparison Direction

Deutsch-WiPA should not imitate Duolingo, Babbel, DW, VHS, Goethe, or Anki directly.

## Positioning

- Duolingo: habit and gamified repetition.
- Babbel: polished structured courses and dialogues.
- DW: free level-based media-rich German course.
- VHS-Lernportal: official German/integration and Beruf-oriented learning path.
- Goethe: exam-style practice and official-level orientation.
- Anki: powerful spaced repetition but no curriculum.

## Deutsch-WiPA niche

B1/B2 workplace German trainer with local-first progress, articles/plurals, sentence production, polite professional communication, and practical scenarios for applications, offices, complaints, appointments, forms, and bureaucracy.

## Product principle

The product should win by being narrower and more production-focused, not by becoming a generic language app.
